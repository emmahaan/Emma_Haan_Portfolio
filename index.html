What I bring

Creative, customized stories with experience

I tailor every story to its audience. Together, these projects show my experience taking a story from the first draft to a finished event or film.

Writing & storytelling

I write scripts, clues and character stories shaped around a specific audience and setting.

Project management

Event planning
