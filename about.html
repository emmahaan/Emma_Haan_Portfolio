My background

I grew up in a small Iowa town with a loving, supportive community that took great care of its history, so there was always something new to discover about the place I called home.

One of the biggest influences on me was my grandma, who was deeply involved in our community and volunteered to help preserve its history. I love that idea, and I want to help preserve history and stories in my own way too.
